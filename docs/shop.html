NotesPro shop

Catalog, cart, checkout.

Put a shop in a markdown fence. Visitors add items and choose PayPal, Visa, or Mastercard on the page. Card numbers are never stored.

Open the shop → How checkout works Watch 25 seconds

Shop promo

Twenty-five seconds: products, a cart, then the payment question. No third-party soundtrack.

Checkout asks for payment

The shop owner keeps PayPal, Visa, Mastercard, and Konto in Settings. A public page does not show that screen. The buyer still picks how to pay.

PayPal

Opens the merchant checkout

When the shop has a PayPal email or paypal.me name, Checkout can open that payment page. The order is mailed to the shop.

Visa

Transfer from a Konto

Visa asks for the buyer’s IBAN or account and shows the shop Konto. Card numbers are rejected.

Mastercard

Same transfer, no card data

Mastercard uses the same Konto step. NotesPro does not store card numbers.

Public page

Show without login

Page settings can publish the shop. Anyone with the link can read the catalog and check out. The rest of the workspace stays private.

Privacy

This promo page does not collect payment card numbers and does not run a checkout. PayPal, Visa, and Mastercard in the app ask how to pay; Visa and Mastercard take a Konto, not a card number.
