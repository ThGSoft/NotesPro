Livia's Jukebox

Paste a Google Drive folder and play it as albums in the page. Nested folders stay nested. Music keeps going when you open another note.

Play in NotesPro → How Google Drive works

Google Drive is the library

Share a Drive folder once. NotesPro lists the audio, keeps the folder tree as albums, and plays through your signed-in account. This page has no ads.

Share the folder

In Google Drive, share the music folder as Anyone with the link can view. Nested album folders work the same way.

Paste the link

In Edit, drop the Drive URL on the jukebox or paste it and click Add Drive. A file link adds one track; a folder link imports the tree.

Pick an album

Folder names become albums (Jazz, Jazz / Bebop). Song titles show the folder the file sits in, plus the filename.

Play it like a diner cabinet

Google Drive first. Local MP3s if you want them. No advertising on this promo page or in the player.

Google Drive

Folders stay albums

A shared Drive folder is the catalog. Child folders keep their names, so the album list matches the library you already keep on Drive.

Google Drive

Signed-in playback

Audio streams through NotesPro while you are logged in. Share the items so anyone with the link can view; no extra music store, no ads.

Albums

Continuous or random

Play one album or everything: continuous or shuffled. Previous and Next sit beside the album picker.

Clips

30 s · 60 s · 90 s

Random-length buttons skip to another track after that many seconds — useful when you are sampling a Drive folder.

Display

Standalone & fullscreen

A note that is only a jukebox fills the preview. Use ⛶ for the whole monitor, or Standalone to hide the NotesPro sidebar and play like a dedicated player.

Background

Keeps playing

Change notes and the song continues. A bottom bar has Previous, Pause, Next, and Stop until you return to the jukebox.

Local too

Drop files or folders

MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG, FLAC. A dropped folder keeps structure as albums, same idea as Drive. Titles use folder and filename.

No ads

This jukebox page does not load advertising scripts and does not show ads. It does not collect payment card numbers.

Google Drive is your music storage. NotesPro only lists and plays files you share with a link. Open the live app to sign in; this page does not create an account by itself.
