Questions

What happens if I do not buy a license?

Nothing you already wrote is locked. You can keep evaluating NotesPro locally and you can keep using the hosted demo. A public production website needs the single-site license.

Is NotesPro free?

The source is published so you can inspect it and run it on your computer for evaluation. The single-site license is 49 EUR once.

Is there a subscription?

No. Monthly and yearly billing are not sold. The Site plan is one payment for one production website.

How do I pay?

Checkout is inside NotesPro. PayPal, Visa, and Mastercard are offered there. This page does not collect card numbers. Visa and Mastercard ask for a Konto and transfer to the shop Konto configured in the app.

Are there setup fees or hidden fees?

No setup fee is added on this page. You pay the license price shown in the app at checkout.

Does one license cover every device?

The license is for one production website, not one laptop. People who use that site sign in there. Evaluating on your own machine does not use up that site.

Can I run it on more than one website?

One purchased license covers one production website. A second public site needs its own license.

I still have questions. How do I reach you?

Open an issue on GitHub. NotesPro is published by ThGSoft.
